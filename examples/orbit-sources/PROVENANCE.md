# ORBIT synthetic fixture

All footage, the still image and music were procedurally authored in this workspace for the 5 October 2026 prototype. No stock media, external API, AI video model or third-party music was used. Visual source: scripts/create-orbit-sources.mjs; musical source: src/audio.ts (kineticGrooveWav). These original assets may be used, modified and redistributed under the separate MIT license in examples/orbit-demo/LICENSE. They are synthetic technical/demo fixtures and do not prove artistic quality or demand.

Jade: 960x540, 24 fps, 9 s. Amber alternative: 960x540, 60 fps, 9 s. Field: 720x720, 25 fps, 8 s. Still: 1200x900 PNG. Music: original 20-second stereo PCM score. Source video has no audio.
